About Us

Your setup
Your style
Your space

Lumen Nook was created for people who love technology but do not want to spend hours trying to understand confusing specifications, endless product lists, and complicated PC-building terminology.

We want to make choosing computer hardware easier by combining useful information, inspiration, and products in a space that feels welcoming whether you are an experienced PC builder or putting together your very first setup.

Instead of treating every computer like a collection of specifications, we focus on helping people create setups that suit the way they actually play, work, create, and spend their time.
